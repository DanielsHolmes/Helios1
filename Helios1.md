## **The Challenge**

On paper, the deal had a lot going for it: strong land equity, liquidity, credit, permits, and a well-budgeted project.

But the borrower had never completed a ground-up build on title. For a transaction of this size, that became the key underwriting challenge. Helios1\_Case\_Study\_Saratoga 1

---

## **What Helios1 Saw**

The missing ground-up track record was only one part of the story.

The borrower had already demonstrated execution through land sourcing, entitlement, permitting, and the way the construction relationship had been structured.

Helios1 Capital looked at those details together and focused on how the borrower actually operated. Helios1\_Case\_Study\_Saratoga 1

---

## **The Outcome**

Helios1 Capital secured the financing and structured the initial loan with a **24-month term**.

The relationship then continued with an additional **\$4M+ facility** for the second Saratoga lot, bringing the documented financing across both lots to **\$8M+**. Helios1\_Case\_Study\_Saratoga 1
