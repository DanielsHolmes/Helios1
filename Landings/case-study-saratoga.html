GROUND-UP CONSTRUCTION • SARATOGA, CA

Financing a First-Time Ground-Up Borrower

The borrower had strong liquidity, significant real estate experience, permitted land, and a clear construction plan.

The challenge was a gap in completed ground-up experience on title. Helios1 Capital looked beyond that single point and evaluated the full opportunity.

The Challenge

On paper, the deal had a lot going for it: strong land equity, deep personal liquidity, prime Silicon Valley location, full city permits in hand, and a well-budgeted project structure.

However, the borrower had never completed a ground-up residential build on title. For a multi-million-dollar transaction in an affluent market like Saratoga, CA, standard institutional guidelines flagged this as an automatic disqualifier, creating the key underwriting hurdle.

02

What Helios1 Saw

The missing ground-up track record was only one part of the story. Standard underwriting filters missed the reality of how the sponsor operates.

The borrower had already demonstrated execution through land sourcing, entitlement, zoning approvals, architectural permitting, and the way the general contractor relationship had been contracted.

Helios1 Capital looked at those details together and focused on how the borrower actually operated.

Key Risk Mitigants Identified by Helios1:

✦
Land Entitlement Mastery: The sponsor successfully guided raw parcels through Saratoga’s rigorous municipal approvals and full permit issuance.
✦
Experienced GC Alignment: Retained an established, bonded general contractor with dozens of completed luxury builds in Santa Clara County.
✦
Robust Balance Sheet: Verified $10M+ net worth and substantial liquid reserves, ensuring buffer against potential cost inflation.
✦
Desirable End-Product Comps: High demand for newly built single-family homes in Saratoga provided significant downside equity margin.
“Strong deals are not always obvious at first glance. Sometimes the opportunity is in the details, the execution, and the full picture behind the borrower.”
Helios1 Capital Underwriting Philosophy
03

The Outcome & Expanding Partnership

Helios1 Capital secured the financing and structured the initial construction facility with a 24-month term, accommodating realistic build schedules and inspections.

Facility Expansion Across Multiple Lots

The lending relationship didn't stop with the first parcel. Following successful site mobilization, the relationship continued with an additional $4M+ facility for the borrower’s second Saratoga lot, bringing documented financing across both lots to $8M+.
