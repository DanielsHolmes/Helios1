Executive Leadership

Rishi Sangal

Founder & CEO

Rishi Sangal brings more than 20 years of Wall Street experience across fixed income trading and mortgage capital markets.

His career includes leadership in residential mortgage-backed securities (RMBS) trading at BNP Paribas, along with roles at BlackRock, RBS, Performance Trust, and Odeon.

That background gave him a close view of how credit is evaluated, how risk is priced, and how capital moves through different market environments.

At Helios1 Capital, Rishi brings that experience into real estate financing, working directly with borrowers and applying institutional market knowledge to individual transactions.

Advisory & Consulting

Thomas Carey, CFA

Senior Consultant

Thomas Carey brings more than 25 years of experience across capital markets, structured finance, investor relations, and business development.

His career has included senior roles at Credit Suisse, BNP Paribas, and Société Générale, with deep experience across mortgage-backed securities, structured products, institutional capital, and alternative credit.

At Helios1 Capital, Tom works with real estate investors, developers, and strategic partners across acquisitions, refinances, rental portfolios, renovations, and new construction.

His role combines financial experience with something equally important: staying close to the borrower, understanding the objective, and helping keep the conversation clear from start to finish.
