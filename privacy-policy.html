Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

How Helios1 Capital collects, protects, uses, and discloses your personal information, along with our compliance standards and communication terms.

Effective Date: June 5, 2025 • Last Updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed by Helios1 Capital ("Helios1", "we", "us", or "our") when you access the helios1cap.com website or other online or mobile services, applications, or communications used to interact with you ("Services").

The Policy has been compiled to better serve those who are concerned with how their Personally Identifiable Information ("PII") is being used online. PII, as described in US privacy law and information security, is information that can be used on its own or with other information to identify, contact, or locate a single person, or to identify an individual in context. Please read our privacy policy carefully to get a clear understanding of how we collect, use, and protect PII in accordance with our website and lending services.

1. Collection of Information

We only collect information you voluntarily and directly provide to us. For example, we collect information when you complete a loan inquiry or contact form submission; sign up for mailing lists, promotional communications, updates or other types of communications; access and use our services; engage with us via social media platforms; engage with us via live chat; and when you email, call or otherwise communicate with us. We will never sell or rent this information to anyone.

The types of information we may collect include:

  • Contact details: First name, last name, physical address, phone number, email address, and ZIP code.
  • Real estate & deal details: Property addresses, project scope, photos, videos, plans, underwriting documentation, demographic information, and financial preferences.
  • Digital communications: Social media usernames, inquiry messages, feedback, and any other information you choose to provide.

In some cases, we may also collect information you provide about others (such as co-borrowers, partners, or broker contacts). We will use this information to fulfill your specific transaction requests and will not send communications to your contacts unrelated to your requests, unless they separately consent to receive communications from us.

2. Information Collected by Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to collect information about you and your interaction with our Services, including information about your browsing behavior, engagement with loan programs, and site navigation.

We, along with third-party vendors such as Google, use first-party cookies (such as Google Analytics cookies) and third-party cookies or identifiers together to compile aggregate data regarding user traffic and interactions on our website to improve our user experience and platform performance.

Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. You can usually choose to adjust your browser settings to remove or reject cookies; however, note that some features of our website may not function properly without cookies.

3. Use of Information

We use the information we collect about you for a variety of legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Facilitate, underwrite, and improve your experience using our financing and private lending Services;
  • Communicate with you about Helios1 Capital loan programs, term sheets, quotes, products, services, market updates, upcoming events, and relevant financing opportunities;
  • Respond directly to your comments, questions, and loan inquiries, and provide dedicated borrower and broker customer support;
  • Monitor, analyze, and optimize website trends, usage, and conversion performance;
  • Personalize the Services and deliver content or financing features tailored to your real estate investment profile;
  • Process transactions and send related loan information, including notices, receipts, or documentation requests;
  • Link or combine with other verified information received from third parties (e.g., public records, property data) to better evaluate financing opportunities; and
  • Carry out any other purpose conveyed to you at the time the information was collected.

U.S. Jurisdiction: Helios1 Capital is based in the United States and the information we collect is governed by U.S. law. By accessing or using our Services or otherwise providing information to us, you consent to the processing and transfer of information in and to the U.S. and other applicable jurisdictions.

4. Sharing of Information

We are the sole owners of the information collected when using our Services. We only collect information that you voluntarily and directly provide. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share personal information about you outside of Helios1 Capital under the following strict conditions:

  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: In response to a formal request for information if we believe disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or legal process;
  • Corporate Structure: Between and among Helios1 Capital and any current and future parent entities, affiliates, subsidiaries, and companies under common control and ownership;
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who perform operations on our behalf (such as title companies, escrow officers, appraisers, and secure hosting providers); and
  • With Your Explicit Consent: Whenever you expressly authorize us to share information with a designated third party.

5. Security & Data Protection

Helios1 Capital takes reasonable and robust measures—including administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—to protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

All sensitive communications and financial data transmitted through our web portals utilize industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS).

6. Your Choices & Opt-Out Rights

Updating Your Information: You may review, update, or correct any personal information you have provided to us at any time by emailing us at support@helios1cap.com or info@helios1cap.com.

Promotional Communications: You may opt out of receiving promotional emails from Helios1 Capital by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link located at the bottom of any marketing email.

Mobile & SMS Opt-In Data: SMS opt-in data and mobile phone numbers will NOT be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

Analytics Opt-Out: You may prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics across websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Your California Privacy Rights (CalOPPA)

The California Online Privacy Protection Act ("CalOPPA") requires commercial websites and online services to post a conspicuous privacy policy. In accordance with CalOPPA, we agree to the following standards:

  • Users can visit and browse our website anonymously without registering;
  • Our Privacy Policy link includes the word "Privacy" and is clearly accessible from our website footer on every page;
  • Users will be notified of any material changes to this Privacy Policy directly on our Privacy Policy page; and
  • Users can change or request deletion of their personal information by contacting our support team via email.

8. CAN-SPAM Act Compliance

The CAN-SPAM Act is a federal law establishing rules for commercial email and giving recipients the right to have emails stopped. To ensure full compliance with CAN-SPAM, Helios1 Capital commits to:

  • Never use false, deceptive, or misleading subject lines or email header addresses;
  • Identify messages as an advertisement in a clear and reasonable manner;
  • Include the valid physical postal address of our business headquarters in all email footers;
  • Monitor third-party email marketing platforms and automation systems for ongoing compliance; and
  • Honor opt-out and unsubscribe requests promptly within statutory timeframes.

9. SMS Messaging Terms of Use

By opting in to or using a text message service provided by Helios1 Capital, you agree to these SMS Terms & Conditions. This agreement is between you and Helios1 Capital, located at 16192 Coastal Highway, Lewes, Delaware 19958, County of Sussex.

Privacy & Security: We only use the phone number and information you provide through SMS services to transmit your requested mobile updates and respond to inquiries. Mobile information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Costs & Frequency: Helios1 Capital does not charge for sending SMS messages; however, standard message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply. Message frequency varies based on your active transactions and interaction with our lending team.
